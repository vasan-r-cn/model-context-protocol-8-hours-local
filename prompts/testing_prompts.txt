Read-only tools

get_interfaces
1. "What interfaces does Router1 have and what are their states?"
2. "Are any interfaces down on the router?"
3. "Give me a table of interface names, IPs, and status."

get_router_info
4. "What IOS version is Router1 running?"
5. "How long has the router been up? What's the hostname and serial number?"

dynamic_show (allowlist test — key lab)
6. "Show me the routing table on Router1." → show ip route
7. "Any OSPF neighbors up?" → show ip ospf neighbor
8. "What's the ARP table look like?" → show arp
9. "What time does the router think it is?" → show clock
10. "Run show running-config on the router." → must get rejected (not on allowlist) — good demo of guardrail + LLM retry behavior
11. "Show CDP neighbors and tell me what's connected."

snapshot_config
12. "Take a snapshot of the current router config."
13. "Backup Router1's configuration before we make changes."
